Privacy Policy — Camilla Ferrari Board Organizer

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Camilla Ferrari Board Organizer ("the app") is a private tool made by Camilla Ferrari for one purpose: to help the owner of a Pinterest account organize her own boards. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the account owner authorizes it through Pinterest, the app reads the account's boards, board sections and Pins (titles, descriptions, images and the board or section each Pin belongs to). It does not access messages, followers, ads, payment information or any other Pinterest account.

How the information is used

Only to create boards and board sections in the owner's account and to move the owner's Pins between them, according to a plan the owner has reviewed. The app does not publish, comment, follow, like or message.

Where the information is kept

On the owner's own computer only: the access token, and a log of each Pin moved (Pin ID, the board it came from and the board it went to), so every move can be undone. Nothing is sent to any server other than Pinterest's own API.

Sharing

No information is sold, rented or shared with anyone. No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are used.

Retention and deletion

The local files are kept only while the organization work is in progress and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps.
